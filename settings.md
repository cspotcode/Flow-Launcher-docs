### Settings

Use the settings to configure and customise Flow and its plugins, and to browse the Plugin Store.


There are three ways to open the settings:

1. Type `settings` in the Flow Launcher search bar and choose "Tweak Flow Launcher's settings".

![Settings via search bar](/assets/Flow_settings_launch.png)

2. Right-click the Flow Launcher system tray icon and choose "Settings".

![Settings via taskbar](/assets/Flow_settings_taskbar.png)

3. Open Flow Launcher, right-click anywhere in the search bar, and choose "Settings".

![Settings via search bar right-click](/assets/Flow_settings_launch_right_click.png)
